## 📌 Analytical Breakdown

### 1. 📈 Descriptive Analysis

- **Sales vs. Target:** During this two-week review period, total sales reached **₦47.37M**, achieving **97.85%** of the targeted revenue (**₦48.41M**).
- **Sales Volume:** A total of **15,000 items** were sold across all branches, with an average daily sales value of **₦9.47K**.
- **By Branch:** The highest sales figures were recorded in **Ikoyi** and **Gbagada**, generating approximately **₦9.8M** each.
- **By Product:** **Hot Pepperoni Feast** was the undisputed top performer, generating **₦4.2M** in revenue.

### 2. 🔍 Diagnostic Analysis

- **By Date:** Daily performance remained consistently close to sales targets throughout most days. However, noticeable gaps occurred on specific dates (notably **December 15: 6.92%** gap), while select days successfully surpassed targets.
- **Branch Performance:** **Surulere** and **Lekki** recorded comparatively lower sales volumes than the other operational branches.
- **Pizza Performance:** **Pepperoni Feast** recorded the lowest revenue (**~₦3.63M**), signaling an immediate need for product-focused promotional bundles and customer incentives.

### 3. 🔮 Predictive Analysis

- **1-Week Forecast:** The forward projection for one week following December 15 reveals a steady, gradual sales increase ranging between **₦3.35M and ₦3.9M per day**.
- **Market Outlook:** The revenue trend demonstrates operational stability with promising growth upside provided current momentum and promotional strategies are sustained.

### 💡 Strategic Conclusion

Domino’s Pizza Nigeria attained a highly satisfactory sales benchmark during this period, finishing remarkably close to target (97.85%). Operational performance can be further optimized by introducing promotional campaigns for underperforming offerings like Pepperoni Feast while reinforcing the supply and marketing of best-sellers like Hot Pepperoni Feast.
